Projects

Heraklion Weather

v1.2.0

A native Android app with real-time weather for Heraklion, Crete. Beyond the forecast it shows sea conditions, wind and meltemi, Saharan dust and air quality, weather alerts and simple tips for the day. Built from scratch with Jetpack Compose and MVVM on the free Open-Meteo APIs.

  • Kotlin
  • Jetpack Compose
  • Material 3
  • MVVM
  • Coroutines
  • Retrofit
  • WorkManager
  • Open-Meteo
View source on GitHub
  • Hourly and 14-day forecast, with a temperature and rain chart
  • Comparison with yesterday and this day last year
  • Sea temperature and waves, with a “good day for a swim?” verdict
  • Wind on the Beaufort scale, plus a meltemi indicator
  • Air quality and Saharan dust for the next 24 hours
  • Alerts for heatwaves, strong wind and very high UV
  • Home screen widget and opt-in daily notifications
  • Animated sky, offline cache, Greek and English, light and dark theme

esx_companyjob

A trucking company for FiveM roleplay servers

A Lua resource for ESX servers on FiveM. It adds a complete company job: shared convoys, trailer deliveries tracked by the server, a company depot and garage, uniforms and employee management.

  • Lua
  • FiveM
  • ESX
  • MySQL
  • Discord webhooks
View source on GitHub
  • Convoys of up to eight players, with rewards that grow with each delivered trailer
  • Trailers created and tracked on the server, from Paleto Bay to the Los Santos docks
  • Shared vault for items and weapons, plus a company garage
  • Four job grades, each with its own uniforms and permissions
  • Boss menu for employees and company funds
  • Optional Discord audit logs, per category
